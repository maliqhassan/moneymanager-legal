# Deleting your MoneyManager account and data

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

This page explains how to delete your MoneyManager account and the data linked to it.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open MoneyManager and go to **Settings → Privacy & data**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes:

- your account (your Google email address, name, profile picture and account identifier);
- all of your financial data in the cloud — accounts, transactions, categories, tags, budgets, bills, goals, recurring payments and notes;
- your receipt photos;
- the data stored on that device.

It also deletes your subscription record at RevenueCat. It cannot be undone. Signing in with Google again later starts a new, empty account.

**Deleting your account does not cancel a MoneyManager Pro subscription.** Cancel it in **Google Play → Subscriptions** so you are not charged again.

## Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email **devopsmarkaz@gmail.com** from the Gmail address you signed in with, with the subject **"Delete my MoneyManager account"**. We will delete your account and all of the data listed above within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is kept, and for how long

- Nothing linked to your account is kept after deletion.
- Copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups until those backups expire, after which they are removed automatically.

## Using the app without an account

If you never signed in, we hold no data about you — everything is stored only on your device. Delete it with **Settings → Privacy & data → Erase data**, or by uninstalling the app.

In the free version, Google AdMob may hold advertising data linked to your device's advertising ID. Reset or delete that ID in **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads**.

## Delete only some of your data

You can delete individual transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, bills and goals in the app at any time. Deletions sync to the cloud when you are signed in.
