# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

MoneyManager is built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

## 1. Using the app without an account

Everything you enter is stored only on your device. We don't receive any of it, and it is not backed up. Erasing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it permanently.

## 2. Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in, Google shares your email address, your name and profile picture, and an account identifier with us. We use them only to create your account and recognise you when you sign in. We never see your Google password.

## 3. Your financial data

This includes the accounts, transactions, categories, tags, merchants, notes, budgets, bills, goals, recurring payments and receipt photos you add. It is stored on your device so the app works offline. With MoneyManager Pro and a Google sign-in, it is also synced to our cloud database so it is backed up and available on your other devices.

## 4. How we use it

Your financial data is used only to provide the app's features to you: storing, syncing and showing it. It is never used for advertising, never shared with advertisers, and never sold. We don't use analytics tools.

## 5. Ads in the free version

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device and app information. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK) you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Your financial data is never shared with AdMob. MoneyManager Pro has no ads.

See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 6. Subscriptions

MoneyManager Pro is bought through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase details from Google Play (product, dates and status) and an identifier for you: a random one for this install, or your account identifier once you sign in — never your email or financial data. We never see your payment details.

## 7. Services we rely on

| Service | What it does | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Hosts our cloud database, file storage and sign-in service | Your account and synced data (Pro, signed-in users only) |
| Google | Sign-in, Google Play billing and AdMob ads | The sign-in request; purchase details; ad data described above (free version) |
| RevenueCat | Subscription status | Purchase details and an app identifier |
| Frankfurter | Provides exchange rates | Only currency codes and dates — never amounts or other details |

Like any internet service, these providers can see your device's IP address when the app contacts them.

## 8. Device permissions

The camera and photo library are used only when you attach a receipt. Notifications are used only for reminders you turn on, and they are scheduled on your device.

## 9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). In the cloud, each record is locked to your account so no other user can read it. Your sign-in session is kept in your device's secure storage.

## 10. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. **Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account** permanently deletes your account, your cloud data, your receipt photos and your subscription record at RevenueCat. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel it in **Google Play → Subscriptions**. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed when those backups expire. Signing out removes your data from that device. Without an account, **Settings → Privacy & data → Erase data** deletes everything on the device.

You can also ask us to delete your account by email — see [Account & Data Deletion](./data-deletion.md).

## 11. Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your data in the app, export it from **Settings → Export data**, and delete it at any time as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under data protection law; contact us to use them.

## 12. Children

MoneyManager is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## 13. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we will update the date above, and we will tell you in the app about significant changes.
